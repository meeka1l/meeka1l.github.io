You may opt-out of personalized advertising through your Android device settings.

4. GDPR (European Users)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), we rely on your consent to process personal data for advertising purposes. You can withdraw or manage your consent at any time via the "Ads" section in your Google account settings.

5. Children’s Privacy

The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. Human Talisman is intended for an audience capable of engaging with atmospheric horror themes. If you are a parent and discover your child has provided us with information, please contact us immediately.
